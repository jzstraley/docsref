## Multifocal Atrial Tachycardia (MAT)

### Definition
* **≥3 different P wave morphologies** prior to QRSs, Irregularly irregular rhythm
* HR typically between 100-150
* Multiple discrete p wave morphologies (multiple discrete p waves) with increased automaticity irritated by increased atrial pressures or hypoxia
* Pneumonia, Hypoxemia, Catecholamine Surge (Sepsis), Pneumonia, hypomagnesemia, theophylline

### Clinical Context
* Clear iselectric intervals between p waves, and the atrial rate is slower
* Usually caused by severe COPD or acute illness
* Pulmonary hypertension, coronary disease, valvular disease, hypomagnesemia, theophylline

### Symptoms
* Typically asymptomatic

### Treatment
* **Treat Underlying Condition** (generally slower)
    * CCBs/Metoprolol long-term
    * Acute Exacerbation: IV Metoprolol/Verapamil (CCBs)
    * Amiodarone/Ablation/Cardioversion do work in MAT
* **Electrolyte management**
    * Correct magnesium deficiency (check and replicate)
