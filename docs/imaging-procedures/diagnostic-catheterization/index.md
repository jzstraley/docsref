Diagnostic cardiac catheterization provides invasive assessment of coronary anatomy, intracardiac pressures, cardiac output, valve hemodynamics, shunts, and coronary physiology.

## Core Components

Diagnostic catheterization includes:

- Vascular access and catheter manipulation
- Coronary angiography
- Left- and right-heart pressure measurements
- Cardiac output measurement
- Pulmonary and systemic vascular resistance
- Shunt assessment
- Valve gradients and valve area calculations
- Assessment of constrictive and restrictive physiology
- Invasive coronary physiology
- Recognition and management of procedural complications

## How Catheterization Answers Clinical Questions

| Clinical question | Principal invasive assessment |
|---|---|
| Coronary anatomy | Coronary angiography |
| Intracardiac filling pressures | Right- and left-heart catheterization |
| Pulmonary hypertension | PA pressure, PCWP, cardiac output, and PVR |
| Low-output state | Fick or thermodilution cardiac output |
| Intracardiac shunt | Oximetry run and Qp:Qs |
| Aortic or mitral stenosis | Simultaneous pressures and valve area calculation |
| Constriction vs restriction | Simultaneous RV/LV pressure assessment |
| Intermediate coronary stenosis | FFR or non-hyperemic pressure ratio |
| Coronary microvascular disease | CFR and microvascular resistance assessment |
