Echocardiography provides real-time assessment of cardiac anatomy, function, hemodynamics, and blood flow. It is usually the first-line cardiac imaging modality because it is portable, widely available, and does not require ionizing radiation.

This section is organized as a practical reference for acquiring, interpreting, and reporting adult transthoracic echocardiograms.

## Interpretation Framework

A complete interpretation should answer six questions:

1. Was the study technically adequate?
2. What is the left ventricular size and systolic function?
3. Is there important valvular disease?
4. What are the right-heart findings and estimated pressures?
5. Is there another clinically important abnormality?
6. Comparison to previous studies?

Interpret findings in the context of the indication, rhythm, blood pressure, loading conditions, image quality, and prior studies.
