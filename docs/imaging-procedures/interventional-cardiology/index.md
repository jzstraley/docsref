Interventional cardiology encompasses coronary angiography, percutaneous coronary intervention, invasive hemodynamics, mechanical circulatory support, and catheter-based treatment of structural and vascular disease.

This section focuses on procedural planning, technical execution, complication management, and the evidence supporting contemporary interventional practice.
