A practical starting point for medical students and interns learning clinical medicine and cardiology.

The goal of this section is to focus on the information that is most useful early in training: recognizing common problems, presenting patients clearly, developing a basic approach to ECGs, and knowing when to ask for help.

## Focus

Early training should prioritize:

- Recognizing sick vs stable patients
- Building a focused differential diagnosis
- Understanding common inpatient problems
- Presenting information clearly
- Learning basic ECG interpretation
- Recognizing common cardiac emergencies
- Knowing when escalation or consultation is appropriate
